Method: open approximation of JdK RS-Ratio and RS-Momentum

The exact JdK RS-Ratio and RS-Momentum formulas are proprietary to RRG Research and have not been published, so this chart uses a documented approximation. For each ETF, with N = 14 periods (weeks, or days in Daily mode):

RS = 100 × ETF close ÷ SPY close · RS-Ratio = 100 + (RS − meanN(RS)) ÷ sdN(RS) · ROC = 100 × (RS-Ratio ÷ previous RS-Ratio − 1) · RS-Momentum = 100 + (ROC − meanN(ROC)) ÷ sdN(ROC).

Means and standard deviations are rolling over the last N periods (population standard deviation). Weekly values use each week's last close; the current week uses the latest close. Quadrants split at 100 on both axes. Values will differ from StockCharts' official RRG.

Method and assumptions
  • Cash flow. Free cash flow (operating cash flow − capex), minus stock-based compensation by default. Companies whose capex is mostly growth spending can use owner earnings (operating cash flow − depreciation − SBC) because its reported FCF has been negative through its capex build-out.
  • Revenue. Year 1 = analyst consensus (low / average / high for bear / base / bull), annualised from the latest reported period when the consensus year ends 6+ months out. After year 1, growth starts at no more than 20 / 30 / 40% and fades each year toward terminal growth (decay 0.55 / 0.70 / 0.80). Ten forecast years, then a Gordon terminal value at 2.0 / 3.0 / 3.5% growth.
  • Margins. Base = median of the last 5–6 periods (mean for cyclicals, so troughs count). Bear and bull sit halfway from the base to the worst and best period. The current margin converges to the scenario margin with a one-year half-life, because margins mean-revert.
  • Discount rate. WACC with CAPM cost of equity = live US 10-year yield + adjusted beta × equity risk premium (Damodaran's implied ERP, editable). Beta is Google Finance's, Blume-adjusted (0.67β + 0.33) and capped at 0.6–2.0. Debt costs the 10-year + 1% after 21% tax. Bear +0.75 points, bull −0.75 points.
  • Per share. Equity = enterprise value + net cash, converted to US dollars at today's rate, divided by live market cap ÷ price (ADRs use ADR-equivalent shares).
  • Special cases. Banks use residual income (book value + excess return on equity). Cyclicals use through-cycle margins. Names with no positive cash-flow history get a reverse DCF only and no published value. Funds are valued at NAV.
  • Confidence. Low when historical margins spread more than 3× the base margin or source data is inconsistent; these use the speculative MOS threshold.
  • Data. Fundamentals and consensus from stockanalysis.com (S&P Global Market Intelligence data), saved with an as-of date and refreshed weekly. Prices, market caps, betas and FX from Google Finance via the Valuation Inputs sheet. Each day's values are saved so changes can be split into price moves and fundamental revisions.
