## Product claims and costs

The legacy Projection Lab is an art-directed illustration. The earlier 1B label represented **zero measured billion-path executions**. The revised banner identifies five illustrative windows. The actual Monte Carlo runner offers 10,000, 100,000 and 1,000,000 requested paths, tracks completions and cancellation, and seeds its generator for reproducible chunks. The model uses 252 trading days, common and stock-specific shocks, dated event jumps and a 15% storm regime. It displays empirical percentiles conditional on assumptions; calibrated real-world probabilities and tail forecasts have not been demonstrated. Benchmark vol, cash rate and missing holding risk can use model assumptions. A new guard prevents holdings beyond the 25-position model limit silently becoming cash. Local single-holding, 10,000-path core timing was 217 ms on this runner; worker/UI latency, a full million-path resource profile and low-end mobile endurance remain unmeasured. [Engine](../public/core/engine.js) · [Numerical regressions](../api/_simstream.test.js)

The live Premium Council sends a single evidence package to one successful model, which writes investor, analyst, pessimistic, allocator and optimistic perspectives and a CEO synthesis in one structured response. Fallback may make a second provider attempt. There is no independent panel vote or multi-model consensus. DEMO scenarios are pre-written. The updated navigation, pricing and classic plan copy disclose the model and path limits.
