A successful build, a completed test checkout, or a change in configuration does not close an acceptance criterion. Each item below requires its own evidence. ## Owner and legal review

| Decision | Approval evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Vendor and AI terms | Signed or otherwise documented display, derived-data, export and AI-input/output rights, retention settings, subprocessor terms, territories and attribution. Verify hosting plan permits commercial operation. |
| Privacy | Identify controller, lawful grounds, notices, purposes for holdings/statement uploads and AI processing, processors, cross-border transfers, retention of audit logs/backups and access/deletion procedures. Evidence of staging isolation does not settle privacy compliance. |
| Payments and consumers | Named merchant, recurring THB total including applicable taxes, renewal date, downgrade/cancel effective date, failed-payment grace, refund policy, complaint channel and response owner. Check actual receipts and portal before publication. |
| Investment research and advertising | Have counsel assess whether personalized portfolio analysis constitutes regulated advice and whether licensing, suitability or geographic limits apply. Model bands and sample probabilities must be labeled hypothetical, and the single-model Council must be described accurately. |
| Jurisdictions and tax | THB pricing suggests reviewing Thailand consumer rules, PDPA, Thai SEC perimeter, VAT and invoices with Thai counsel/accountant. Confirm entity domicile and customer locations. For US, EEA, UK or other sales, obtain the relevant adviser, privacy, tax and consumer review before offering service there. |
| Operations and incidents | Name billing/support and incident contacts, escalation and complaint routes. Document access revocation, provider outage communications, evidence preservation, jurisdiction-specific breach notification thresholds and deadlines. Rehearse the process. |

No new purchase is authorized. Limit or postpone sale where necessary until an owner can approve a compliant arrangement.
